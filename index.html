Elite Prompting Techniques

Modern AI power users use structured prompting systems instead of basic questions. These techniques are commonly recommended across Reddit communities, AI research papers, prompt engineering guides, and operational AI workflows.

🧩

Chain-of-Thought

Ask AI to reason step-by-step before generating the final answer.

“Think step-by-step before generating the final ASO strategy.”
🎭

Role Prompting

Assign AI a specific expert identity to improve quality and relevance.

“Act as a senior TikTok Shop growth strategist...”
📝

Few-Shot Prompting

Provide examples of good outputs before requesting new ones.

“Here are 3 successful titles. Create 10 more similar versions.”
🔁

Iterative Prompting

Top AI users refine outputs multiple times instead of accepting the first answer.

“Improve this further for higher CTR and emotional impact.”
📋

Structured Prompting

Use sections like Context, Task, Constraints, and Output Format.

Context → Task → Constraints → Output
🪞

Echo Prompting

Ask AI to rewrite vague prompts into better prompts before answering.

“If my request is vague, improve the prompt before answering.”

Advanced Prompt Engineering

The biggest difference between average AI results and exceptional AI results is prompt quality. Teach teams how to think like AI operators, not just AI users.

🎯

Role Prompting

Always tell AI who it should act as before giving instructions.

“Act as a senior Amazon SEO strategist...”
🧠

Context Prompting

Provide audience, platform, product type, goals, and constraints.

“Targeting parents of toddlers age 2–5...”
⚙️

Output Formatting

Tell AI exactly how the final output should look.

“Generate 10 bullet points under 180 characters.”
Prompt Engineering Masterclass

Better Prompts = Better Results

Good AI Operators vs Weak AI Operators

The quality of AI output depends heavily on how teams interact with AI systems.

❌

Weak AI Usage

• Uses vague prompts
• Accepts first response immediately
• Does not verify information
• No platform-specific context
• Generic outputs
• No iteration or optimization
✅

Strong AI Usage

• Provides detailed context
• Uses structured prompting
• Iterates multiple times
• Gives examples & constraints
• Reviews and optimizes outputs
• Combines AI with business judgment
